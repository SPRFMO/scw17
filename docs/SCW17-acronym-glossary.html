SCW17 Acronym Glossary

Scope

This glossary is a working aid for SCW17 jack mackerel materials and related SPRFMO repositories. It was compiled from local text and report sources in:

  • /Users/jim/_mymods/sprfmo/scw17
  • /Users/jim/_mymods/sprfmo/jjm
  • /Users/jim/_mymods/sprfmo/JM_SCW_prep

It is intended to make draft papers, schedules, MSE notes, and benchmark material easier to read. It is not a controlled vocabulary. Definitions and formal names should be checked against adopted SPRFMO reports before final submission.

Organisations, Meetings, and Documents

Acronym Meaning Use in this repo family
ANJ Area of National Jurisdiction Used in Peru jack mackerel annual-report and CPUE contexts to distinguish national waters from the SPRFMO Area.
CJM Chilean jack mackerel Species/fishery shorthand used in CPUE, acoustic, and benchmark papers.
DHC Direct human consumption Fishery category used in Peru data and selectivity discussions.
EU European Union Member/fleet context, often paired with PFA in offshore fleet documents.
FAO Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations Source of optional MSE training resources linked from SCW17 materials.
IFOP Instituto de Fomento Pesquero Chilean fisheries research institute appearing in CPUE and acoustic source material.
IMARPE Instituto del Mar del Peru Peruvian marine research institute appearing in annual-report and CPUE source material.
INPESCA Instituto de Investigacion Pesquera Chilean fisheries research institute appearing in CPUE standardisation source material.
JM Jack mackerel Short form for the SPRFMO jack mackerel assessment and MSE work.
JMSG Jack Mackerel Sub-group Scientific Committee sub-group context in assessment and annex material.
JMWG Jack Mackerel Working Group Intersessional working group used in benchmark, MSE, and SCW17 preparation documents.
PDF Portable Document Format Rendered document format for public papers and supporting material.
PER Peru Country/member shorthand used in cross-platform and source-data contexts.
PFA Pelagic Freezer-trawler Association Offshore fleet/source context in EU/PFA CPUE material.
PRODUCE Ministerio de la Produccion Peruvian ministry appearing in Peru annual-report and TAC context.
QMD Quarto Markdown Source format for SCW17, SCW16, and related working papers.
SC Scientific Committee SPRFMO Scientific Committee.
SC13 13th Scientific Committee meeting Last year’s Scientific Committee meeting in 2025; source context for the accepted assessment and SC13 technical annex.
SC14 14th Scientific Committee meeting This year’s Scientific Committee meeting in 2026; current target for SCW17 documentation and MSE working-paper contributions.
SCW Scientific Committee Workshop Numbered workshop series used for technical preparation ahead of Scientific Committee decisions.
SCW14 14th Scientific Committee Workshop Earlier workshop context, especially for effort-creep and CPUE discussion.
SCW16 16th Scientific Committee Workshop 2026 benchmark preparation workshop and source repository context.
SCW17 17th Scientific Committee Workshop Current repository and Wageningen MSE workshop preparation context.
SPRFMO South Pacific Regional Fisheries Management Organisation Regional fisheries management organisation responsible for jack mackerel management.
TG Task Group Technical planning group used in SCW17 and JMWG schedules.
ToR Terms of Reference Scope and objectives for benchmark and working-group processes.
TT Task Team Technical task-team context in benchmark and MSE planning documents.
WCPFC Western and Central Pacific Fisheries Commission Regional fisheries management organisation referenced for comparative or contextual material.
WMR Wageningen Marine Research MSE contractor and workshop host context.
WP Working paper Draft or submitted technical document.

TMB Template Model Builder C++/R modelling framework used by RTMB and sdmTMB-related workflows.

Assessment, Reference Points, and Diagnostics

Acronym Meaning Use in this repo family
AAV Average annual variability Performance metric for catch or TAC stability across MSE projections.
B0 Unfished biomass, often unfished spawning biomass Reference biomass used in depletion, SPR, and MSE calculations.
Blim Limit biomass reference point Risk threshold used in reference-point and performance-metric discussions.
BMSY Biomass at maximum sustainable yield Reference point used for status, Kobe/Majuro-style summaries, and candidate performance metrics.
CV Coefficient of variation Measure of uncertainty used for indices, diagnostics, and model summaries.
DEPM Daily egg production method Survey method used in jack mackerel abundance-index context.
F Fishing mortality Fishing-pressure quantity used in assessment and reference-point calculations.
F/FMSY Fishing mortality relative to FMSY Status metric used in Kobe/Majuro-style summaries.
F35% Fishing mortality at 35% spawning potential ratio Candidate SPR-based reference point in SCW17 reference-point work.
FL Fork length Length measure used in biological and fishery data.
FMSY Fishing mortality at maximum sustainable yield Reference fishing mortality used in assessment and management summaries.
MSY Maximum sustainable yield Equilibrium yield reference point calculated from assessment or operating-model quantities.
NASC Nautical area scattering coefficient Acoustic-survey backscatter quantity used in acoustic abundance work.
NLL Negative log-likelihood Model-fitting diagnostic quantity.
P(B<Blim) Probability biomass is below Blim Risk metric used in performance tables and trade-off summaries.
R0 Unfished or equilibrium recruitment Recruitment reference quantity used in assessment and simulation contexts.
S-R Stock-recruitment Relationship linking spawning biomass and recruitment.
SDNR Standard deviation of normalized residuals Diagnostic for index or composition fits.
SE Standard error Standard uncertainty measure reported with estimates.
SPR Spawning potential ratio Reference-point and depletion concept used in F35% and related discussions.
SSB Spawning stock biomass Biomass measure used in assessment, reference points, and MSE outcomes.
SSB_F=0 Spawning stock biomass without fishing Counterfactual unfished spawning biomass used in depletion or reference calculations.
TAC Total allowable catch Catch limit or advice quantity produced by management procedures.

EST Estimator MP component that estimates stock status or index state before the HCR is applied.
HCR Harvest control rule MP component that turns estimated status into TAC or catch advice.
K60 Kobe-green 60% tuning shorthand Tuning target used in candidate-MP comparison material.
PERF Performance metrics Diagram and workflow shorthand for MSE scoring outputs.
TUNE Tuning and comparison Diagram and workflow shorthand for selecting or comparing MPs against targets.
