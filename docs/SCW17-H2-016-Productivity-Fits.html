H2 0.16 productivity-scenario fits

Published

June 4, 2026

SPRFMO

South Pacific Regional Fisheries Management Organisation
Jack Mackerel Working Group

This document compares the relative fits to index and length-composition data for the four two-stock 0.16 productivity scenarios: h2_0.16ls, h2_0.16ll, h2_0.16hs, and h2_0.16hl. The suffix convention is h or l for steepness and s or l for the stock-recruitment year set.
